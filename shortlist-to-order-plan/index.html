Shortlist to Order Plan

Turn multiple candidates into a limited, reviewable plan — with open items marked, not hidden.

What an order plan is

An order plan is a short list of candidates, each recorded as a five-field note, with anything still unconfirmed marked clearly. It is a plan, not an order and not live data.

Keep it limited

A plan you cannot finish reviewing is just a longer list. Keep the number of candidates small enough that every note can be re-checked.

Mark open items

Anything still to confirm — price, option, availability, rules — goes in the check field of the note. If it is open, it is visible. Nothing is left implied.

Margin noteA hidden ambiguity becomes a surprise later. Mark it now.

Building the plan

  1. Take the shortlist from your first pass.
  2. Write a five-field note for each candidate.
  3. Mark every open item in the check field.
  4. Re-check each source page before submitting.
?Open item: does your plan show every unconfirmed detail, or is any of it still implied?

A worked plan

  1. Candidate 1: a bag — variant recorded, price to confirm.
  2. Candidate 2: a jacket — size recorded, availability to confirm.
  3. Candidate 3: sneakers — style recorded, ready to re-check.

Planning example — not an order, live data, or a saved record.
